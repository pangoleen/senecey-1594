# Sign inventory for Colbert 33 ff. 528-529 (Pelissier's hand). ASCII token names.

Each cipher sign gets one ASCII token. The names describe the shape only.
`crops/f530_alpha.png` and `crops/f530_nulles.png` show a period table of the same sign set in another hand.

| Token | Shape |
|---|---|
| `II` | two short vertical strokes, side by side |
| `o-` | small o with a dash to the right |
| `-o` | small o with a dash to the left |
| `th` | small o with a horizontal line through it (dash on both sides) |
| `o` | plain small o (often a dot after it) |
| `#` | hash: two vertical strokes crossed by two bars |
| `V` | small triangle that points down |
| `sq` | small square |
| `xx` | x with a bar through it, often blotted (looks like "ae" struck out) |
| `q` | q or 9 with a straight tail |
| `=` | two or three thin horizontal bars |
| `7` | figure 7 |
| `4` | figure 4 (sometimes with a dot or a cross-bar) |
| `eL` | c or e with a very large loop that hangs below the line |
| `my` | m (three minims) with a tail below at the right |
| `3` | figure 3 or z-like, alone |
| `g` | very small x or r with a dash to the right |
| `oo` | two round o's joined side by side, NO bar on top |
| `bw` | two small loops or feet under ONE top bar (like Greek varpi, or "tt" joined); compare `oo` (no bar), `aa`, `inf` |
| `8` | small slanted 8 or S |
| `8x` | larger 8 or 6 with a loop at the top and a bar through it |
| `vt` | v or u whose right arm goes on as a long flat stroke to the right |
| `ubar` | small v or o that sits under a separate thick bar |
| `xc` | two curves back to back, like a small x or ">c" |
| `10` | the figures 1 0 joined ("io") |
| `xy` | x or r joined to a y with a tail below |
| `ay` | a joined to y with a tail below (round body at the left) |
| `o3` | o joined to a 3 (round body at the left, 3-shaped tail; no long tail below) |
| `ll` | two tall strokes with hooked tops, crossed by a bar (like "ff" or a boxed ff) |
| `tj` | like the letter pi with two bars at the top, leg goes below the line |
| `MM` | horizontal I-beam: two long bars joined by two short strokes |
| `uo` | u joined to o |
| `nr` | looks like "me" or "ur": two or three minims and a small r, on the line |
| `psi` | Greek psi |
| `14` | figures 1 4 |
| `17` | figures 1 7 |
| `b` | o with a tall stem that has a cross-bar (like a b with a crossed stem) |
| `lam` | Greek lambda |
| `tl` | "el" or "tl", e joined to a tall l |
| `acr` | small a with a long bar through it |
| `eps` | Greek epsilon, like a 3 turned round |
| `S` | tall long s, thick |
| `T` | capital T |
| `w` | Greek omega (round w) |
| `4o` | 4 joined to o |
| `rho` | tall sign: small loop at the top, long stem, hook to the left at the foot (like a long g or rho) |
| `u3` | u or m with a 3-shaped tail at the right (like "uz") |
| `uq` | u (two minims) with a straight tail below at the right |
| `aa` | wide double loop (like "oo" or "aa") with a bar through it |
| `phi` | o with a long vertical stroke through it |
| `tz` | t or h with a cross-bar and a zigzag foot (like "tz") |
| `tt` | vertical stroke with two cross-bars |
| `tO` | o with a cross that stands on top of it |
| `rc` | reversed c |
| `Gc` | large circle with a small reversed c inside (like a G or @) |
| `K` | capital K |
| `ec` | small "ee" or "cc" with a tail below |
| `X` | x with a vertical stroke through it |
| `6oo` | a 6 joined to two o's |
| `N9` | 9 or g with a small cross at its foot |
| `NoIo` | o with a cross on top joined to a second o (o, tall stroke, o) |
| `NLeo` | flat sign like "ell" or the Leo sign |
| `7x` | small crossed sign, like a small 7 or x with a stroke (added after pass B; twice on f. 575v) |
| `[flourish]` | pen stroke joined to the next sign, no value (added after pass B) |
| `[blot]` | ink blot or deleted sign |
| `?` | cannot say |
| `inf` | lying 8, the infinity sign |
| `fff` | three short vertical strokes crossed by one bar |
| `dd` | two tall plain strokes crossed by one bar (like "tt" or a tall H); compare `ll`, `tj` |
| `::` | four dots in a square |
| `x1` | plain small x, no bar |
| `uyb` | "uy": u joined to a y with a tail below (like `my` but with only two minims and a y) |
| `77` | two figures 7 side by side |
| `P` | flag: a vertical stem with a small square flag at the top (like P or a boxed gamma) |
| `bb` | compound box sign: "tt" joined to a "b", or a double b in a frame |
| `W` | capital W (often drawn with doubled strokes, like VV) |
| `h` | small letter h |
| `8+` | an 8 or g shape with a horizontal bar through its middle |
| `ee` | two c or e shapes side by side, the second with a tail below; alone (no "my" after it) |
| `eemw` | ONE token for the group: two c/e shapes + "my" (minims with a tail) + a small raised sign (like a tiny w or tt) at the upper right |
| `cmy` | a c or e (often with a dot before it) joined to "my", with NO raised sign and only one c |
| `hf` | a tall hooked f or long s with a curl at the foot, alone |
| `dl` | an o with a stem through it and a loop or curl at the top (like phi with a flourish); compare `phi` (plain o with a straight stroke) |
| `pf` | small p with a hooked top |
| `gq` | g-like sign with a zigzag tail |
| `obar` | small o under TWO thin bars; compare `ubar` (v or o under one thick bar) |
| `A1` | like a capital A or H: two strokes joined by a bar |
| `kh` | small k-like sign with no tail |
| `kq` | k-like or 4-like sign with a long tail below that is crossed |
| `cr` | a c or e with a dot or short stroke before it, alone (added later) |
| `qb` | 9-like sign; in this hand it looks like `q` (added later) |
| `ao` | like `inf` but drawn as an a joined to an o (left loop larger) (added later) |
| `[insertion]` | a place where small signs are written above the line |

Clear French words go in double quotes. Small words written above the line by a later hand are glosses: ignore them.
